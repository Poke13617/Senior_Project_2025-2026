# Senior_Project_2025-2026

What is this project?

- This project is a text-based game where the goal will be to outlast a set timer. The NPCs will be 4 different allies that support you in the same sense as an asymmetrical horror game. The character chosen will have different kits as follows:

  1. survivalist/sentinel - cooldowns: 5/6 (charge-up sprint/attack)
     
     special kit perk: overheal
  
  2. support, sentinels move towards you more - cooldowns: 4/4/7 (heal/small dash/mobility)

     special ability: sprint, allows for triple the movement speed for a single turn during the third ability
    
  3. survivalist (charge attack, leaves user vulnerable) cooldowns: 5/6 (mobility/charge stun)

     special perk: flight, mobility makes this kit invulnerable for half its duration
  
  4. weaker support/attacker cooldowns: 5/5/4 (attack/attack/perk)

     special ability: tarot cards, tarot cards contain certain perks
     
  5. sentinel/attacker - cooldowns: 5/7 (charge attack/self-heal)
     
     special perk: steel plating, this kit takes 30% less damage from attacks, in return, this kit is vulnerable during self-heal and takes 40 damage during the attack. This kit also gets 140 health over the 100 the other kits have.
     
  6. Attacker/self-heal
     
     special perk: This kit reloads after 6 attacks, before this kit runs out of attacks from the 6, it takes 1 turn to reload, along with this, the kit can only be played as a Last Man Standing, which shortens time and gives the perk, making the 6 attacks to 8 attacks.

- All kits will have 2-3 abilities, ranging from a team heal to a long-duration stun. All NPCs that are used will play as independent characters, providing support in areas that your kit needs (for example, support will result in allies playing closer to you while sentinel will result in your team staying less around you and more around your support.)
- If the chance that all 4 allies lose to the enemy, your player character will be thrown in a Last Man Standing (or LMS for short) health will be set to 150 during LMS and certain abilities in the kit will be either stronger or have a better cooldown.
- The attacker kit will be a shorter duration for survival in rounds and will not be supported by teammates, but instead it gets more health and the attack/self-heal are both a shorter cooldown. (normal cooldowns are 5-7 turns, attacker will be 3)
- There will be 3 different enemies
